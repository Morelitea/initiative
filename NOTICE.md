# Third-party artwork

Initiative is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3 (see `LICENSE`). A few of the
profile decorations under `frontend/public/decorations/` are not our drawings.
They are used as published rather than redrawn, and they keep their own terms.
Each file also carries its credit in an SVG `<metadata>` element.

## Public domain

These carry no conditions; they are listed so the drawings are credited to the
people who made them.

- **`banners/books-desk.svg`** — the writer is *Thinking Woman* by **j4p4n**,
  from [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/), released to the public domain.
  Baked to a banner-sized image; the room, the light and the shelves are ours.
- **`banners/books-shelf.svg`** — the shelf of books is a public-domain line
  drawing supplied for this pack. Baked to a banner-sized image; the paper it
  sits on, the colour in the bindings and the light are ours.
- **`banners/pets-catnap.svg`** — a crop of a public-domain drawing of a cat,
  re-encoded at banner size. The original is fifteen thousand paths and five
  megabytes, which is not a thing to send anybody for a profile banner.
- **`banners/pets-fetch.svg`** — a public-domain drawing of a dog, on the black
  it was drawn on, baked to a banner-sized image.
- **`trophies/plants-morel.svg`** — the morel is *Morel* by **platzster**, from
  [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/), released to the public domain. Only
  the disc behind it and its colour are ours.

- **`trophies/firstnations-handprint.svg`** — the hand is
  [Red hand](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_hand.svg) by
  **mjchael**, from Wikimedia Commons.
- **`trophies/disability-wheelchair.svg`** — the International Symbol of Access,
  from [Wheelchair symbol](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wheelchair_symbol.svg)
  on Wikimedia Commons. Original design by Susanne Koefoed for Rehabilitation
  International.
- **`trophies/disability-chronicpain.svg`**, **`trophies/disability-mentalhealth.svg`**
  — the awareness ribbon is
  [Black ribbon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Black_ribbon.svg) from
  Wikimedia Commons, recoloured.

Everything else under `frontend/public/decorations/` is original work for this
project. Where a pack draws on a living design tradition, the manifest says so:
those are original compositions in that tradition, not reproductions of any
community's, family's or maker's own design.
