# Your space

Most of Initiative is organized by community and initiative. But you also get **personal views** that gather *your* things from across **every** community you're in, so you never go hunting community by community.

Click the **Initiative logo** in the **top-left corner** — it sits above your communities on the rail.

## My Tasks

Everything assigned to you, from every community and project, in one list. Group it **by date** or **by community**, filter by priority, status, or community. This is the page to live in when you just want to know what you should be doing.

## Tasks I Created

The tasks *you* made, wherever they ended up. Good for chasing things you handed off.

## My Projects

Every project that has reached *you*, across all your communities — a fast way to jump to one without switching communities first.

"Reached you" means the same thing on all of these pages: shared with you directly, shared with a role you hold, or shared with everyone in an initiative you're in. It is **not** "everything you could open if you went looking" — a community admin, who can reach anything in their community, still gets a list of their own work rather than the community's whole inventory.

## My Documents

The documents that are yours, on the same rule.

## My Contacts

The people you can actually talk to, on one page — so you can find a colleague without first remembering which community you know them from.

The page is built in sections:

- **Your connections** — the people you've mutually agreed to stay in touch with.
- **People outside your communities** you've agreed to message.
- **Each of your communities**, in the order you dragged them into the rail, collapsible.

Each community section lists the members who **can message you and who you can message** — not everyone in the room. A community you're alone in is left out entirely, since there'd be nobody to list.

!!! info "Starting out empty is normal"
    New accounts start on the **Private** message setting, which means nobody can ask to message you yet — so your community sections will be empty at first. The page says so, and offers the one setting that fills them. See [Who can reach you](messages.md#who-can-reach-you).

It reads as one table: the same columns — person, name, and the communities you have in common — run down every section, so somebody in three of your communities lines up with themselves. Hover the small community icons on a row for the full list.

- **Star anyone** to keep them at the top, including people you share no community with. Starring is private and tells them nothing.
- **One search box** covers the whole page. Each community keeps its own member list, so a search visits them one at a time and says so while it works.
- Each section **pages on its own**, twenty at a time, without shoving the others around.
- **Waiting requests** — connection or message, either direction — sit above the page, so you don't have to go to Settings to answer one.

Opening a row takes you to [that person's profile](../account/profile-and-preferences.md#your-profile-page). The actions menu on a row is where you act on somebody where you found them: **connect**, **ask to message**, **ignore**, or remove a connection. See [Messages](messages.md).

## My Messages

Your private conversations — one to one and end-to-end encrypted. The one page here whose contents live on your device rather than on the server.

See [Messages](messages.md) for who can reach you, connections, and what happens to your history when you sign out.

## My Calendar

Your events **and** dated tasks from every community, on one calendar (same "reached you" rule as above). The single best view of your actual week.

## My Stats

A friendly look at your own activity:

- **Current streak** — consecutive days you've got things done.
- **On-time rate** — how often you finish before the due date.
- **Tasks completed** — this week and all-time.
- **Velocity** — how much you take on versus finish each week.
- An **activity heatmap** of the past year.

It's just for you. Encouragement and a sense of your own rhythm, not a leaderboard, and nobody else sees it.

## Favorites

Star a project to add it to **Favorites** for one-click access. Personal to you; starring changes nothing for anyone else.
