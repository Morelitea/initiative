# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask most. Each one links to the fuller explanation.

## Getting in

### I can't find the community I was told to join

Most communities are private and don't appear anywhere until you're invited. Ask whoever told you about it for an invite link — that link is the way in, and it takes you straight there after you create your account.

If your server runs a [community directory](guides/communities.md#finding-a-community-to-join), listed communities show up under **Join a community** on the community rail. A community that isn't listed won't be there, however you search.

### My invite link says it's no longer valid

Invite links can expire, be used up, or be turned off by an administrator. None of that is about you. Ask for a fresh one.

### I don't see "Join a community"

The directory is a server-wide feature that starts switched **off**, so plenty of servers don't have one. Communities there are invite-only. See [Finding a community to join](guides/communities.md#finding-a-community-to-join).

## Age

### Why am I being asked my date of birth?

Because you're joining, or already belong to, a community that anyone signed in can find. Communities like that are open to people you haven't met, and you need to be **13 or older** to take part in one.

You're asked once. The answer stays with your account, so the second community you join asks nothing.

### What happens to the date?

We use it to work out whether you're old enough, and then discard it. Your account records **that** you answered — never the date. It isn't sold, shared, or stored anywhere.

### I'm not joining anything public. Why was I asked?

Something added you to a listed community without you clicking Join: an administrator added you, a single sign-on group sync did, an invite you accepted led into one, or a community you were already in listed itself after you joined. Any of those puts you in a community anyone can find, so the question follows.

A community that has **not** listed itself never asks — not when you're invited, not when you're added.

### My account can't message anyone

<a id="my-account-cant-message-anyone"></a>

If your account can't reach people — you can't join communities you can see, and the parts of Initiative that are open to people outside your own communities are closed to you — the usual cause is the age question.

Two things look similar and are not:

| What you see | What it is | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A screen asking your date of birth, and you can't get past it | You belong to a listed community and haven't answered yet | Answer it. You'll be asked once. |
| A screen saying you told us you're not old enough | Your account answered the age question as under 13 | See below — an administrator can reset it |

Everything inside the communities you were **invited** to is unaffected either way. This only closes the parts that are open to people you haven't met.

### I typed my birthday wrong and now I'm locked out

That's the common case, and there's a way back: ask an administrator of your server to **reset the age question** for you. Anyone on the support tier or above can do it from the platform users page, and it's recorded in the audit log like any other action one person takes on another's account.

They can't see the date you entered, because it was never kept. Resetting simply lets you answer again from scratch.

!!! info "Why you can't just re-answer"
    A question you can retry until it comes out right isn't a question. So the answer stands, and undoing it takes somebody else — which is also why the reset is logged.

### Can this be switched off?

Yes, by the platform owner, under **Settings › Admin › Community**. Turning it off is the owner confirming that every account on the deployment belongs to someone 18 or older — which a company or school rollout knows and a public server doesn't. See [Data and compliance](security/data-and-compliance.md).

## Accounts

### Can I change my username?

Not on your own. Your handle is how people find and mention you, so changing it is a moderator action — ask an administrator. Your **display name** is yours to change any time, under [Profile and preferences](account/profile-and-preferences.md).

### Why does my name show in one community and not another?

Each community decides whether it shows real names or just handles. In one that shows handles only, your display name isn't rendered to other members there. See [Communities](guides/communities.md).

### I've been signed out and can't get back in

Changing your password signs out every device — that's deliberate. If you didn't change it, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. If your server uses single sign-on, there may be no password to reset; sign in the way you normally do. See [Signing in](getting-started/signing-in.md).

### My account is suspended

A suspended account can still sign in and reach its own settings, which is how you can be told why. Every community is closed while it lasts. Suspension takes nothing away — memberships, work and everything you wrote stay exactly where they are, and lifting it restores the account whole. Ask an administrator of your server.

## Getting help

### Something looks broken

If it's the software rather than your account, see [Reporting a problem](security/reporting-a-problem.md). If it's about a specific community — who's in it, what you can reach — ask that community's administrators first; they can see and fix far more of it than the server's operators can.
